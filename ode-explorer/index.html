Expression help

Use u and v, + − * / ^, brackets, pi, e, sin, cos, tan, exp, log (natural), sqrt, abs, tanh, min and max. Write 2*u or 2u. Write u*v explicitly. MATLAB elementwise operators are accepted. Other names are detected as parameters. Supply a baseline, and optionally both limits to enable a slider. pi and e retain their usual constant values; function names are reserved. Any detected name is a constant parameter, including t. The equations are autonomous.

Explore an ODE

  1. Choose one or two equations, then load an example or enter your own model.
  2. Set parameters, the initial condition and the time interval. Press Simulate.
  3. Click in the middle plot to simulate from a new initial condition. For one equation, only the horizontal coordinate u matters.
  4. Keep previous initial conditions to compare solutions. Clicking a trajectory row highlights it in both plots. Remove a curve with its × button.
  5. Use Plot window & display to change ranges, nullclines and arrow density. The time plot fits all solutions automatically.

Choose User defined for blank equations. Parameter names are detected automatically. Enter a baseline and optional minimum and maximum for each. If both limits are blank, the parameter stays fixed at its baseline. Sliders start at the baseline; Reset returns them to it. Moving a slider automatically updates the field, nullclines, equilibria and all retained solutions. Changes to numerical settings apply on the next simulation. Use Apply model & redraw to re-simulate all curves.

Solid blue and dashed orange curves mark the u and v nullclines. Square markers show equilibria; circles show initial conditions. Equilibrium stability and eigenvalues are estimated numerically. In 1D the eigenvalue is f′(uₛ); in 2D both eigenvalues of the Jacobian are shown. A centre of the linearisation does not establish a nonlinear centre.

RK45 controls local error. Absolute tolerance is 10⁻³ times the selected relative tolerance. RK4 uses the maximum step as a fixed step and has no error control. Both methods are explicit; very stiff systems may require a stiff solver.

Integration stops at undefined values, the step limit or |state| > 10⁷. Curves can leave the phase window while remaining visible in the time plot. Each run is limited to 60,000 step attempts, and at most 12 trajectories are retained.

Keyboard: Tab to the phase plot, use arrow keys to adjust the initial condition (Shift for a smaller change), and press Enter to simulate.
