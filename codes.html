Biological Effective Dose calculator

The calculator accompanies the paper Changes in the retreatment radiation tolerance of the spinal cord with time after the initial treatment, by T. E. Woolley, J. Belmonte-Beitia, G. F. Calvo, J. W. Hopewell, E. A. Gaffney and B. Jones.

Important medical disclaimer

This software is a research tool. Thomas E. Woolley is a mathematician and cannot advise on a particular medical application. Any clinical questions or decisions must be directed to the user's local clinical specialists.
