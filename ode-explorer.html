ODE Explorer

Explore how differential equations behave. Choose an example or enter your own equations, click an initial condition and compare its phase trajectory with its time solution.

Open the applet at full size · User guide and source code

Using the applet

Choose one or two equations. In the phase plot, click to set u₀ or (u₀,v₀). Parameter sliders update the field, nullclines, equilibria and all retained solutions automatically. Circular markers show initial conditions, and square markers show equilibria. Eigenvalues appear beside each equilibrium classification.

Select User defined to start with blank equations. Parameters are detected from the names in your equations. Give each a baseline; supply both Min and Max to enable a slider, or leave both blank to keep it fixed. Use PNG and CSV to download plots and solution data.

The examples accompany Modelling with Differential Equations. The full user guide explains the notation, examples and numerical methods. Equilibria and nullclines are numerical estimates; very stiff systems may need a dedicated stiff solver.
