Outreach

Videos, audio, articles and projects that use mathematics to engage wider audiences.

“An idea not communicated can scarcely be said to exist.”

As an academic, I spend much of my time producing mathematics that relatively few people need to read directly. Publicly funded research also needs to be explained clearly beyond academia, so outreach is a substantial part of my work.

Zombies

In 2009, Robert Smith? and collaborators published a mathematical biology paper modelling an outbreak of zombies. The conclusions were bleak: if the dead should rise, there may be little room left for the living.

In 2010, a call went out to mathematical colleagues for chapters in Mathematical Modelling of Zombies. I immediately jumped at the chance. The book was published for Halloween 2014. I also give talks about this work at science festivals and workshops. If you're interested in knowing more, please contact me.
