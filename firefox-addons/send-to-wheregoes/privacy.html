Send to WhereGoes: privacy notice

This independent browser extension submits only the link you explicitly choose to the third-party WhereGoes website for redirect tracing. You can select a hyperlink using the Firefox context menu or submit a URL through the extension popup. When you open the popup, the current page URL may be prefilled for convenience, but is not sent until you press “Trace with WhereGoes”.

The extension does not independently follow tracking links, monitor your browsing, store browsing history, collect analytics, or transmit data to its developer. The selected URL is passed to WhereGoes in its webpage, where the website's own privacy practices apply. Tracking URLs may contain personal or one-time information, so avoid tracing sensitive links.

The extension requests only the permissions it needs for the user-initiated context-menu and popup actions, plus access to the WhereGoes website to fill in and submit its tracing form. It does not read the contents of unrelated websites. No account is needed.

WhereGoes is a third-party service. This extension is independent of WhereGoes and is not affiliated with its operators.
